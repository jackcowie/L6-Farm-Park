Welcome to Sunshine Farm Park, a fun countryside attraction located between Edinburgh and Carlisle. Enjoy a relaxing family day out with friendly animals, woodland walks, a play park, café and farm shop. Whether you are planning a full day of activities or looking for a pleasant stop during your journey, there is something here for everyone.
