Sunshine Farm Park is conveniently located between Edinburgh and Carlisle, making it an ideal destination or stopping point for travellers. Free parking is available beside the main entrance, including accessible parking spaces. We are open Monday to Sunday from 9:30 am until 5:00 pm, although opening times may change during winter and public holidays.
