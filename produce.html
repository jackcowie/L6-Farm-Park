Our café serves a tasty selection of breakfasts, sandwiches, soups and homemade meals throughout the day. Younger visitors can choose from our children’s menu, while our cake counter offers delicious treats and freshly baked scones. Vegetarian and gluten-free options are available, and our friendly staff will be happy to provide information about food allergies.
